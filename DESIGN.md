# FULATAN COMMUNICATION UI Direction

The app follows the supplied FULATAN reference: blue primary branding, orange accent, rounded white cards, product grid, category shortcuts, cart/checkout flow, seller/contact actions, chat, profile and a separate admin control center.

## Customer app
- Home: logo, welcome, search, hero promotion, categories, hot deals.
- Categories: brand catalogue.
- Product details: gallery, price, condition, specs, seller, Call, WhatsApp, Chat, Buy Now.
- Cart: quantity controls, subtotal, delivery, total and checkout.
- Checkout: delivery or office pickup, address, secure payment placeholder.
- Favorites: saved products.
- Profile: orders, favorites, chats, language, settings and secure logout.

## Admin app
- Dashboard: totals, activity overview, quick actions.
- Products: add, hide/publish and delete.
- Contacts: Call number, WhatsApp number, Live Chat toggle.
- Security: admin-only UI plus server-side Firebase claim/rules.

The reference image is treated as a design direction, not a source of copyrighted assets.
